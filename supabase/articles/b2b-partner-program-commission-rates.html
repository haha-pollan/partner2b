TL;DR Most B2B partner programs do not tell partners how they get paid. Of 622 programs in the Partner2B directory, 339 (55%) publish no commission model. Where a program states a commission rate, the median is 20% for referral fees, 20% for revenue share and 20% for resale discounts. Referral programs are the most open (87% publish a model). Technology programs are the least open (25%).

By Elena Zap, founder of Partner2B. Updated 22 November 2026.

What is a partner program commission?

A partner program commission is the payment a vendor makes to a partner for bringing in or closing business. In the programs we reviewed it takes three forms:

  • Referral fee: a payment to a partner who introduces a customer. Listed by 148 programs (24%). See referral program.
  • Discount on resale: a reseller buys at a reduced price and keeps the margin. Listed by 119 programs (19%).
  • Revenue share: the partner receives a share of what the customer pays over time. Listed by 76 programs (12%). See revenue share.

A program can list more than one model, so these counts overlap.

How many partner programs publish their commission?

Based on the public program pages listed in the Partner2B directory on 22 November 2026, 283 of 622 programs (45%) publish how partners are paid. The other 339 (55%) do not.

Referral programs are built around one promise, a payment for an introduction, so the payment is the first thing they state. Technology programs are mostly about integrations and co-selling. Their economics are often agreed one partner at a time, which is a likely reason three in four say nothing. That is our reading of the numbers, not something the data proves.

What does not publishing a commission cost a program?

A partner deciding where to spend time compares programs. If one page says 20% and the next says nothing, the second one needs a call before it can be compared, and many partners will not make that call. The program that states its terms gets considered first.

Publishing does not mean giving up flexibility. A program can state a standard rate and say that larger partners can negotiate.

How to read a partner program commission page

  1. Find the model: referral fee, resale discount or revenue share.
  2. Find the number, and what it applies to (first-year revenue, all revenue, one-time deals).
  3. Check how long the payment lasts and when it is paid.
  4. Look for tiers. 65% of programs in the directory have tiered benefits, so the rate you start at may not be the rate you can reach. See partner tiers.
  5. Check for deal registration. Only 13% of programs offer it, and it is what protects your commission when two partners reach the same customer. See deal registration.

Method and limits

This article uses public information from the 622 published programs in the Partner2B directory, pulled on 22 November 2026. All programs are currently marked as not verified, so the figures describe what programs state publicly, not what they pay in practice. Commission data is mostly a label, not a number, and percentages are extracted from text. Region data is almost entirely "Global", so there is no regional comparison. The same method will be repeated each year so the numbers can be compared.

FAQ

What are typical B2B partner program commission rates?

Where programs state a percentage, the median is 20% across referral fees, revenue share and resale discounts. Individual programs range from under 2% to 60% for referral fees.

Which type of partner program is most likely to publish its commission?

Referral programs. 89 of 102 (87%) state how partners are paid.

Why do so many technology partner programs not publish commission?

Only 25% of technology programs state a model. Many of them are integration and co-selling arrangements where terms are agreed with each partner, so there is no standard rate to publish. That is an interpretation, not a finding.

How should a vendor present commission on its program page?

Name the model, give the rate or the range, say what it applies to and for how long, and say how tiers change it.

Where can I compare partner program commissions?

The Partner2B directory lists 622 programs across 29 product categories, with commission models, tiers and features side by side. Vendors can claim a listing for free and correct their terms.
